Helpful to know

Questions before we talk?

Here are a few simple answers to help you prepare your first enquiry.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Tell us your name, company, preferred contact method, coal requirement, approximate quantity, timing, and destination. Even a short message is a good start.

Can I ask about a recurring supply?

Yes. Mention that the requirement is recurring and share the approximate rhythm or schedule you have in mind so the conversation starts with useful context.

Where is Khaqan Coal Company based?

We are based in Darra Adam Khel, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan, and are proud to work from the heart of our local coal community.

How will the team respond?

Our preview form is ready to collect your enquiry. Once a phone, WhatsApp, or email connection is added, the team can respond through your preferred channel.
